# USCIS H-1B Filing Checklist (synthetic)

This internal checklist is applied to every H-1B petition before it is filed with USCIS. It is a synthetic summary for demonstration purposes and is not legal advice.

### 4.1 Prevailing wage
The offered wage stated in the petition must be greater than or equal to the prevailing wage listed on the certified Labor Condition Application (LCA).

### 4.2 Worksite
The beneficiary's worksite ZIP code must be one of the worksite ZIP codes listed on the certified LCA.

### 4.3 Filing window
The petition may be filed no more than 180 days before the requested employment start date.
