Section 1. Scope
These guidelines apply to all matters handled for Globex Industries by outside counsel. Invoices that do not comply will be reduced.

Section 2. Time Entry Requirements
2.1 Increments. Time must be recorded in increments of one-tenth of an hour (0.1).
2.2 Block billing. Each time entry must describe a single task; entries that combine multiple tasks will be rejected.
2.3 Narrative detail. Narratives must contain at least five words and may not use "attention to file" or "review documents" without further detail.
2.4 Daily limit. No timekeeper may bill more than 12 hours to Globex matters in a single day.

Section 3. Staffing and Rates
3.1 Conferences. Internal conferences between firm timekeepers are not billable.
3.2 Paralegal rate. Paralegal time may not be billed at a rate above $250 per hour.
3.3 Clerical work. Clerical tasks such as filing, copying, scheduling and data entry are not billable regardless of who performs them.

Section 4. Expenses
4.1 Value. All fees must reflect work that is reasonable, necessary and of value to Globex given the stage of the matter.
