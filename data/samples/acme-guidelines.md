# Acme Corp Outside Counsel Billing Guidelines (synthetic)

These guidelines govern all invoices submitted by outside counsel to Acme Corp. Invoices must be submitted monthly in LEDES format with UTBMS task and activity codes, and every time entry is reviewed against the requirements below before payment is approved.

### 3.1 Block billing
Each time entry must describe a single task. Entries that combine multiple tasks and exceed 0.5 hours will be rejected.

### 3.2 Vague narratives
Narratives must be specific. Entries under five words, or using "attention to", "work on file", or "review documents" without identifying the subject, will be rejected.

### 3.3 Staffing
No more than two timekeepers may bill for attending the same deposition or meeting (activity code A109 or narratives mentioning deposition/meeting attendance) on the same day.

### 3.4 Travel
Non-working travel time is billed at 50% of the timekeeper's standard rate.

### 3.5 Clerical tasks
Filing, copying, scheduling, and document organization are clerical and not billable regardless of timekeeper.

### 3.6 Daily cap
No timekeeper may bill more than 10 hours on this matter in a single day.

### 3.7 Reasonableness
All time billed must be reasonable and necessary to the representation.
